# Provenance

Every candidate has a record distinguishing independently selected manual/community words, project fixtures, verified spelling work, and third-party-derived material. Individual ordinary words may omit a source reference when accompanied by a contributor assertion and license basis. Third-party material requires a reference and license notes; bulk sources are explicit and never treated as independent contributions. Third-party derivation never implies CC0 eligibility.

Stable pseudonymous actor identifiers avoid requiring names or email addresses. Provenance records must link to candidates and are audited before release.
