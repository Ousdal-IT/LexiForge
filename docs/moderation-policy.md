# Moderation policy

Reviewers explicitly resolve commonness, readability, pronunciation, spelling, neutrality, proper-name, brand, abbreviation, and passphrase-suitability criteria as `yes`, `no`, `unknown`, or `not_applicable`. Shared policy defines which must be `yes` for approval.

Allowed transitions are centralized: submitted to review/rejected/automatic rejection; review to approved/rejected; approved to superseded/withdrawn; rejected back to review. Automatic rejection can only return to review through an explicit override and cannot jump to approval. History is append-only and the latest transition must agree with candidate status.
