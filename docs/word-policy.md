# Word policy

Future production candidates should be common, easy to read, pronounce, and spell, neutral, not proper names or brands, and suitable for memorable passphrases. These require human, language-specific moderation.

M0 automates structural properties only. It makes no unsupported claim that a word is common, harmless, neutral, correctly classified, or linguistically suitable. Optional project-maintained blocklists provide an explicit future hook, not a substitute for moderation.
